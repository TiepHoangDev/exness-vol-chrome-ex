1. Name:
Trade Stats & Tools for Exness Web

2. Short Name:
ExTradeStats

3. Summary (Description < 132 chars):
Enhance Exness WebTerminal with multi-account stats, one-click closing, and real-time profit/loss tracking.

4. Description (Long):
Take your trading on the Exness WebTerminal to the next level with this essential toolkit. Designed for serious traders, this extension provides an advanced, integrated dashboard that gives you critical insights and powerful controls right where you need them.

Key Features:
*   **Multi-Account Management:**
    *   Instantly switch between your active trading accounts with a simple dropdown menu.
    *   Statistics and actions are automatically updated for the selected account.

*   **Advanced Real-Time Statistics:**
    *   View total profit and loss for your entire account at a glance.
    *   For each instrument (e.g., XAU/USD), see a detailed breakdown of:
        *   Total Profit & Loss
        *   Total Buy & Sell Volume
        *   Average Buy & Sell Prices

*   **Powerful One-Click Trading Actions:**
    *   **Close All:** Instantly close all open positions for the selected account or a specific instrument.
    *   **Close Profit:** Close only the profitable positions.
    *   **Close Loss:** Close only the losing positions.

*   **Seamlessly Integrated UI:**
    *   A clean, draggable floating panel that you can place anywhere on your chart.
    *   Adjustable font size and a responsive design that expands to fit all information without truncation.
    *   A "Live" status indicator shows you when data is being polled in real-time.

How to use:
1. Log in to Exness WebTerminal.
2. The extension toolbar will appear automatically.
3. Drag the toolbar to your preferred position.
4. Use the "Close Profit" button to manage your trades efficiently.

Privacy Policy:
This extension operates entirely on the client side. It reads trading data directly from the Exness WebTerminal API using your active session. No personal data, trading history, or credentials are sent to any third-party servers.

Disclaimer:
This is an unofficial utility extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Exness. Use at your own risk.
